Lemon barley soup with greens

35 minServes 4One pot

A bright, filling soup for the middle of the week. The barley cooks straight in the broth, so there is one pot to wash, and the lemon goes in at the very end so it stays fresh.

Use any sturdy greens you have: chard, kale or the tops of a bunch of beetroot all work.

Ingredients

  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 150 g pearl barley
  • 1.5 l vegetable stock
  • A large handful of greens
  • 1 lemon, zest and juice
