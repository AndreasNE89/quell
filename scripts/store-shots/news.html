Ferry line returns to the old harbor after forty years

Two electric boats will cross the bay every twenty minutes from spring, and the first tickets cost less than the bus.

By Maren Holt · 6 min read

For the first time in four decades, commuters on the north shore will be able to reach the old harbor without crossing the bridge. The council approved the new line on Tuesday night by nine votes to two.

Ad

The two boats are battery powered and charge at the harbor quay between crossings. Each carries up to 120 passengers and forty bicycles, and the crossing takes eleven minutes.

“People kept asking why the water was the one route we never used,” said the transport chair, who rode the test crossing with a group of students from the harbor school.
