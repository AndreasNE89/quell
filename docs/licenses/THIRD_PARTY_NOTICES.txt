Filter lists
------------

The rulesets, cosmetic rules and scriptlet data under generated/ are compiled from these lists.
StampStack translates their rules into Chrome's Declarative Net Request format and drops the
rules that format cannot express.

EasyList, EasyPrivacy
  https://easylist.to
  GPL-3.0 (GPL-3.0.txt) or CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

  https://github.com/easylist/easylistchina
  GPL-3.0 (GPL-3.0.txt) or CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

EasyList Cookie List
  https://easylist.to
  CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)

uBlock Origin filters: Ads, Badware risks, Unbreak
