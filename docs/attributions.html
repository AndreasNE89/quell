Attributions

Third-party data and services used by StampStack.

StampStack compiles third-party filter lists into its packaged rulesets at build time. Those lists are the work of their respective projects and remain under their own licenses. StampStack does not modify their intent — it translates the rules into Chrome's Declarative Net Request format, dropping any rule the format cannot express.

Scriptlets and redirect resources

StampStack's scriptlet library and redirect stubs are original implementations written against the behavior documented by uBlock Origin's resources library; no uBO source is copied into this extension.

Sponsor segment data

Sponsor segment data is provided by the SponsorBlock community project and is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. StampStack queries it only with a 4-character hash prefix of the video id — never the video id or the page URL.
